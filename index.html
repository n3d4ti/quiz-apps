Study Tips for Success

🎯

Focus on Weak Areas

Identify words you struggle with and practice them repeatedly until they become second nature.

📖

Learn in Context

Don't just memorize definitions - understand how words are used in sentences and different contexts.

🔄

Practice Daily

Short, frequent practice sessions are more effective than long cramming sessions before the test.

✍️

Write It Down

Writing words and their meanings helps reinforce memory and improves spelling accuracy.

Track Your Progress

Each practice session helps you get closer to test success. The quiz will track your performance and highlight areas that need more attention.
